#### A. Conformal 3-state output (`python/oxydrop_ml/conformal.py`)

* Input: the final hybrid decision logit `z` of a window (> 0 = low oxygen) and `p = sigmoid(z)`. Label: `low` (window-mean reference SpO2 < 90), windows with t >= 60 s, as in P1.
* Nonconformity score `s(x, y) = 1 - p_y` (`p_1 = p`, `p_0 = 1 - p`). The prediction set of a window is `{y : s(x, y) <= q_y}` with the finite-sample level `ceil((n + 1) (1 - alpha)) / n` of the calibration scores.
* **Primary method: class-conditional (Mondrian) split conformal**: one quantile `q_0`, `q_1` per class from the calibration windows of that class. Control: marginal split conformal (one `q`). In one dimension both reduce to two thresholds `a <= b` on `z`: set = {0} when `z < a`, set = {1} when `z > b`, otherwise both or neither.
* **3-state map**: set {0} = **Normal**, set {1} = **Possible low oxygen**, set {0, 1} or {} = **Unsure**. Only Unsure abstains.
* Miscoverage targets: the curve uses `alpha` from 0.005 to 0.5 in steps of 0.005; the declared headline operating point is **alpha = 0.10**, with 0.05 and 0.20 reported next to it. Nothing is selected on the outer test people.
* **Calibration sets (nested, training people only).** For outer person `o`: the five other Hoffman people `q`. Each `q` is scored by the pipeline selected for `o` (`source`, per-user calibration and `k` of `hybrid_p1_final_choices.csv`), whose CNN logits come from the inner fits that exclude `{o, q}` and whose meta-learner is fit on the four remaining inner people (leave-one-inner-person-out). The windows of `o` never enter. `o` itself is scored by the outer pipeline exactly as in `scores/hybrid_final_hoffman.csv`.
* **Shipped parameters (`app/data/conformal.json`)**: the pipeline that `export_hybrid.py` deploys (`hybrid_weights.json`), scored out of fold on all six Hoffman people (leave-one-person-out meta-learner on the six out-of-fold tables), pooled. These parameters are not evaluated on any person (no held-out person is left); their nested evaluation is the row above.
* Reported on the outer persons, pooled (windows) and per person: coverage (share answered), accuracy and balanced accuracy on answered windows, sensitivity and specificity on answered windows, the share of **all** hypoxic windows alerted, confident-error rates per class, empirical set coverage per class next to the target `1 - alpha`, the share of the three states, ECE before (all windows) and on answered windows. Person-bootstrap 95 % intervals (people resampled, B = 2000, seed 0). Windows overlap by 80 % and people differ, so the exchangeability behind the guarantee does not hold across people; the report shows the empirical coverage on the held-out person next to the target and says where it misses.
* **Matched-coverage comparison with v1 and v2**: v1 abstain band 93.9 % accuracy at 84.4 % coverage, v2 94.7 % at 68.0 % (`results/eval_v1_vs_v2.csv`). The accuracy of the conformal output is read from the nested curve at the coverage of each (linear interpolation in coverage, Mondrian and marginal) and, as a coverage-exact control, from the confidence-ranked curve (most confident windows by `|z|`, no calibration set). The same quantities are computed for the shipped v1 chain band and v2 on the same windows (`scores/baseline_P1_hoffman.csv`). Differences carry a paired person-bootstrap interval; all are exploratory.

#### B. OpenOximetry SaO2 contact-domain test (`python/oxydrop_ml/openox_spo2.py`, Daytona only)

* People: the 34 OpenOx people of `train.openox_holdout` (deterministic, stratified by Monk group). They are excluded from SSL (`ssl_full.pt` manifest: `n_excluded_subjects` 34), from the stage-B warm-up and from every supervised fit of the hybrid members (the `Pool` default removes them before any sampling). A unit test checks the held-out list against the exclusion. Of the 34, 25 people have clock-aligned recordings (34 recordings), 24 have SaO2 labels, 1 has Monk 7+. Skin-tone gaps on this set are therefore very imprecise and the report says so.
* Models: final R members of the deployed hybrid (`hyb_final/R_s{0,1,2}.pt`, mean of the three seeds, primary), the P members (comparison), and `stageB_full.pt` (OpenOx warm-up only). Output: the contact-domain SaO2 head `aux_spo2` with domain 1 (contact red/IR), no baseline embedding needed. Norm folded for domain 0 in the final checkpoints is undone exactly for domain 1. No fine-tuning, no recalibration and no threshold on any held-out person.
* Labels: `sao2` = the contact display series mapped to ABG SaO2 with the per-encounter linear map fitted on that encounter's blood-gas draws (`data/openox.py`). Windows with t >= 60 s of aligned recordings. **Primary label: lag-corrected** `relabel(ws, lag*)` with `lag*` from `estimate_lag(training OpenOx people, method='ppg')`, i.e. learned on the OpenOx people outside the held-out set only, with a bootstrap interval over encounters; if the peak is not confident (score <= 0.05) the primary label stays at lag 0 and the report says so. Lag 0 is reported next to it, and the `abg` lag method as a sensitivity row. The models were trained with lag-0 labels; this disadvantages them slightly and is stated.
* **Direct-ABG secondary label**: the windows whose span contains a blood-gas draw marker (`sao2_draw`), one value per draw (mean prediction of those windows). This label is lag independent and not a model of the reference.
* Metrics: MAE, bias (prediction minus SaO2), ARMS (root mean square error, FDA convention, over SaO2 70 to 100 and over all windows), AUROC for SaO2 < 90 using the predicted SpO2, per person and pooled; mean +- SD over people, 95 % person bootstrap (B = 2000, seed 0).
* Baselines on the same windows: **train-mean** (mean SaO2 of the training OpenOx people, windows t >= 60 s, at the lag of the test labels), **textbook ratio-of-ratios** `110 - 25 R`, `R = exp(abs_ror_RG)`, clipped to [70, 100], and **calibrated ratio-of-ratios** (OLS of SaO2 on `abs_ror_RG` fit on the training OpenOx people only). The contact oximeter display (`spo2_ref`) against SaO2 is reported as the device reference. A win over the best of the two baselines is only described as a win if the paired person-bootstrap interval of the MAE difference excludes zero; otherwise tie or loss.
* **Skin tone**: Monk 1-3 / 4-6 / 7+ (hand-site median) and ITA tertiles (tertile cut points from all OpenOx people with an ITA, label free). Per-group person-mean MAE, ARMS, bias, AUROC, dark-minus-light gaps with independent person bootstraps (darkest minus lightest ITA tertile; Monk 7+ minus 1-3 where the group has at least 3 people, otherwise "not estimable"). The SpO2-minus-SaO2 bias of the contact reference by skin tone uses ALL blood-gas draws of all OpenOx people with a device reading and skin tone (no model involved; person bootstrap). Textbook ratio-of-ratios by skin tone uses all aligned OpenOx people (no fitted parameter) and is labelled as such.
* Limits stated now: contact finger-clip red/IR sensor, not a phone camera; healthy volunteers on a staircase protocol; 24 labelled held-out people; label lag uncertainty; the stored SaO2 is the display mapped to ABG, not the ABG itself, except for the draw rows. "466 desaturations in 144 people" cannot be traced in the data; the report states the counts the data has.

#### C. Heart rate voting (`python/oxydrop_ml/hr_vote.py`)

* Estimators per window: spectral harmonic-sum `s` and autocorrelation `a` (`python/oxydrop/hr.py`, channel chosen as in `baselines/hr_est.select`, shipped gate), and the CNN HR `c` (seed median of the FEAT-006 LOSO runs; true leave-one-subject-out for BUT and MTHS). `s` and `a` have no learned parameter.
* Rule, fixed here: (1) a tolerance `tol(v) = max(5 bpm, 6 % of v)`; (2) if at least two of `{s, a, c}` agree within the tolerance, the answer is the mean of the largest agreeing group (a tie goes to the group that contains `c`); (3) otherwise harmonic and subharmonic correction: each estimate is moved to the octave `{x/2, x, 2x}` closest to `c`, the vote of step 2 is repeated, and if nothing agrees the answer is `c`; (4) octave check of the result against the CNN: when the voted value is within tolerance of `2c` or `c/2` and not of `c`, the answer is `c`; (5) causal median of the last 5 answers of the same recording. All values stay in 40 to 200 bpm. Every step is also reported alone (voting only, + harmonic correction, + octave check, + causal median) so the contribution of each is visible; none was tuned.
* Data: BUT PPG v2.0 (38 people) and MTHS (62 people), the same windows as P2; **clean** = the shipped v2 quality gate passes, **all** = every window. Report MAE, within 5 bpm, doubling and halving rates, coverage, per-subject spread (SD, min, max of per-subject MAE) with person-bootstrap intervals, next to the CNN alone, the spectral estimator, and the pre-registered P2 final rule. Target stated before any result: MAE < 3 bpm and doubling < 1 % on clean clips; whether it is met is reported either way. MTHS rows stay in the cache (licence), only aggregates are committed. BUT recordings contribute one window per clip, so the causal median is a no-op there and the report says so.
