#### B. The three candidates (full LOSO by person, both hands together, every selection nested on the five training people)

Common: windows of 10 s, 2 s hop, t >= 60 s scored, the first 60 s of each recording is the label-free per-recording baseline; training on clean recordings (plus the candidate's own disclosed augmentation); the held-out person's whole recording, baseline minute included, is the object that is degraded in the stress test; models are not refit per level. Score files `results/photon/scores/<model>__L0..L5.csv` and `<model>__X1.csv` in the format of `CONTRACT.md`. TM is computed from `pred` (the forced decision at full coverage), never from `answer`. Three seeds; the headline is the mean TM of the three seeds (never the best seed) and ablations count as separate models.

1. **PhotonTwin-Inverse** (`photon/twin_inverse.py`). A small encoder maps the observables of a window (baseline-relative ln DC and ln |AC| per channel, ln ratio-of-ratios RG, RB, GB, perfusion index, window SNR) to the physical state of the finger: arterial saturation, blood volume, melanin, pulsatile swing and the source-to-detector separation rho as a latent, so flash-to-lens geometry is inferred and not assumed. The differentiable PhotonTwin renderer (`optics.py`, band-integrated Farrell diffusion with published spectra) decodes the state back to the observables. Loss: heteroscedastic reconstruction + Huber(saturation against the training reference) + BCE(SpO2 < 90) + prior KL on the latents. A small residual head carries the cues the optics cannot see (heart-rate rise, chain deltas) and is L2-regularised; its share of the logit is reported. The reconstruction likelihood gives a posterior width on saturation that widens when light is weak and drives abstention (threshold from nested inner CV, target answered accuracy >= 0.90). Controls: `twin_inverse_noresid` (no residual head, physics path only) and `twin_inverse_null` (the same pipeline fed observables of another person aligned to t, or constant observables). Guards from the red-team: every scaler, latent prior width and spectral correction is fitted inside the outer fold, with an assertion that the held-out person is absent; the shifted-label run (each training person's label trace circularly shifted by >= 200 s) must give TM 0.50 +- 0.05. The ratio-of-ratios green/blue choice comes from the optics table above (not blind, see A).
2. **FlashGap** (`photon/flashgap.py`). Chain-style features (the 19 chain v1 fusion deltas and dHR) plus SNR and quality features (spectral SNR at the heart-rate peak per channel, perfusion index per channel, DC level relative to baseline, clipping fraction, beat-detection fraction), trained with physics-sampled distant-flash augmentation: K degraded copies per training recording with d_rho U(0, 12) mm, leak U(0, 0.4), log-uniform read noise and random channel kill (p = 0.15), drawn from an independent RNG stream whose (level, seed) pairs are disjoint from the evaluation pairs (asserted in code and tested). Model: class-balanced logistic regression with feature x SNR interactions and a small LightGBM, family and hyperparameters nested on inner person-grouped CV; clean rows weight 1, augmented rows 0.25. Nested conformal risk control sets `answer` (inner answered accuracy >= 0.90). Controls: `flashgap_noaug` (same features, no augmentation), `flashgap_nosnr` (no SNR features), `flashgap_null` (same pipeline fed observables of another person or constant observables). Out-of-family guard: the X1 stress is not sampled by the augmentation (`degrade_x` is not imported by training code).
3. **OxyTrack** (`photon/oxytrack.py`). A strictly causal state-space tracker: saturation is a slowly varying latent trajectory with a dynamics prior learned from training people; each 10 s window gives a noisy observation (dHR, chain deltas or a low-dimensional projection, per-window SNR) through an emission model pooled from the training people with a per-person offset and scale adapted online without labels (test-time EM), starting from the label-free first minute. Observation noise is set from measured SNR, so in low light the filter leans on its prior, its posterior widens and it abstains. Adaptation only in the first 60 s and on windows with P(normoxic) > 0.95 (red-team fix). Output: causal P(SpO2 < 90) and an 80 % saturation band. Controls: `oxytrack_ema` (chain v1 probability with a causal EMA, span nested-selected: if OxyTrack does not beat it, the gain is smoothing and is called smoothing), `oxytrack_noadapt`, `oxytrack_nosnr`, `oxytrack_null` (the same filter fed constant or other-person observations aligned to t). A unit test proves causality (changing windows k+1.. does not change outputs at windows <= k) and that the EM statistics at window k use only windows <= k.

#### C. The stress test (`photon/stress.py`, `results/photon/HARNESS.md`; a simulation)

A simulation of a phone whose flash sits farther from the lens (the iPhone 18 Pro / 13 Pro problem): only the held-out person's recordings are degraded, by the band-integrated PhotonTwin render at a larger source-detector separation (d_rho = 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 mm for levels 0 to 5, renderer separation rho0 + 0.25 d_rho with rho0 = 3 mm), per-channel DC attenuation and pulsatile gain (blue dies first), auto-exposure gain that raises shot and read noise, an additive non-pulsatile leak (0, 2, 5, 10, 20, 35 %), quantisation to 1/32 DN and clipping. Median red-channel pulse-band SNR 32, 24, 13, 0, -13 dB at levels 1 to 5. The out-of-family stress X1 (level-3 optics plus motion-like DC steps, sample-and-hold frame drops, per-channel gamma 0.75 / 0.80 / 0.85, AR(1) noise) is not sampled by any candidate's augmentation or training. Declared deviations (renderer sign of the image-source term, the effective-separation scale `PATH_SCALE` 0.25, 1/32 DN quantisation) are in `HARNESS.md`; `PATH_SCALE` was fixed from the input-side SNR of the recordings before any model was scored on a degraded recording. The levels are **not** measurements of any phone and the real iPhone behaviour is untested. Because FlashGap's augmentation and the L-levels come from the same simulator family, a FlashGap win at L1 to L5 is nearly guaranteed and is not evidence of generalisation; only X1 and the real L0 data can carry weight.

#### D. The winner rule (fixed here, before any candidate score was read)

1. **Primary metric**: clean L0 real data, Hoffman time-matched balanced accuracy computed from `pred` at full coverage, mean of the three seeds, compared **paired** with `chain_v1` (logistic chain v1, shipped, 0.796) by the person bootstrap (people resampled, B = 2000, same resamples for all models).
2. **"Beats chain" requires ALL of**: (a) the paired person-bootstrap 95 % interval of the TM difference to `chain_v1` excludes 0 on the positive side; (b) at least 5 of the 6 held-out people have a higher own TM than `chain_v1` (exact one-sided sign test reported: 6/6 p = 0.016, 5/6 p = 0.109); (c) the worst person's TM is not below the worst person's TM of `chain_v1` (0.458); (d) the candidate's null control (`*_null__L0` or `*_blank__L0`) scores TM <= 0.55. With only 462 distinct person resamples of six people and four or more comparisons, (a) alone is necessary but not sufficient.
3. **Stress levels L1 to L5 and the out-of-family X1 only break ties.** They are simulations. The secondary criterion is the mean paired TM difference over L2 to L5 and X1 (X1 is the only one that tests more than the simulator family) and the answered accuracy at the candidate's own coverage, compared with random abstention at the same coverage. After that the tie-break is novelty and JS deployability, then the fewest fitted parameters.
4. **If nothing passes**, the shipped chain stays the headline, every candidate is reported as a tie (or loss) with its interval, and the most novel tying model may be exported only as an alternative engine with the stress result as its story, without the word "better". No ablation or control can be promoted to winner after the fact; a model chosen on a later look at its own scores is labelled optimistic.
5. Reporting: all candidates, ablations and controls appear in `TABLE.csv` whether they won or not; per-person TM, worst person and the paired difference are shown. Skin tone: Hoffman has one dark-skinned participant (n = 1), so no skin-tone claim; OpenOximetry skin groups only where n >= 5 and only if the optional OpenOx step of FEAT-002 was run. Selective output: accuracy on answered windows is compared with random abstention at the same coverage because conformal abstention can skip exactly the transition windows that TM rewards.

#### E. Declared guards (leak and metric-fooling checks)

* Shifted-label run for PhotonTwin-Inverse (>= 200 s circular shift, expected TM 0.50 +- 0.05) and the null/blank controls of all three candidates (TM <= 0.55, flagged in `TABLE.md`).
* Fold-internal fitting of every scaler, prior, hull and spectral correction; held-out person absence asserted in code.
* OxyTrack: EM at window k uses windows <= k only (tested); the EMA control shows whether any gain is smoothing.
* FlashGap: augmentation seed streams disjoint from evaluation pairs (tested); X1 held out of every training path.
* TM always from `pred` at full coverage; abstention analysed separately.

Six people, 12 recordings of Nexus 6P phones, one dark-skinned person, a staircase hypoxia protocol of the Hoffman dataset; real SpO2 ground truth from a reference oximeter. All stress levels are simulation. No new phone has been measured. Time-matched balanced accuracy scores only the desaturation transitions (100 s bins with both classes). Nothing here is a medical-device claim.
