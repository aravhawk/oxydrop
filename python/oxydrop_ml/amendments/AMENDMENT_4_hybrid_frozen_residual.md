### Amendment 4 (Nov 15 2026, FEAT-008, exploratory extension: low-variance residual; written BEFORE any outer or inner prediction of the new family existed)

What was seen first. All outer and inner results of the Amendment 2 families `P`, `R`, `D` (3 seeds) and the P1 table that follows from them (selected hybrid 0.779, residual hybrid at default levers 0.809, chain with its threshold refit 0.816, best baseline 0.796). Inner-fold-only checks of a per-user z-score calibration and of non-linear combiners (product, max, min of chain and CNN logit) were run on the seed 0 inner tables and gave no inner-CV gain, so they were not added. The fits of `P` and `R` show a train-vs-test gap (pooled 0.855 vs 0.779 for the selected hybrid), i.e. the fine-tuning of the whole network on at most five hypoxia people is the likely source of variance. That observation motivates the one extension below.

New family `L` (residual hybrid with a **frozen backbone**): identical to `R` (low-oxygen logit = chain prior + network output, same out-of-person prior, MTHS low-oxygen terms off, same inner validation people, same init `stageB_full.pt`, 400 steps), except that only the low-oxygen linear head (73 weights, `lowox`) is trained, every other parameter is frozen, BatchNorm and dropout run in eval mode, and the learning rate is 5e-3 (chosen a priori, not tuned, because the head has few parameters; the recipe value 1e-3 is used by the other families). Seeds 0, 1, 2; 6 outer + 30 inner fits per seed.

Candidates added to step 1 of the selection rule (the CNN-containing set, simplest first, ties inside 0.01 go to the earlier candidate): `L` = `[c + corr_L]` and `c+corrL` = `[c, corr_L]`. Extended order: `P, D, R, L, c+p, c+d, c+corr, c+corrL, all`. Steps 2 and 3 are unchanged.

Status of the result. The Amendment 2 candidate set and its result stay the **confirmatory headline** (`HYBRID_FINAL`). The extended set is reported as `HYBRID_FINAL_EXT` and is **exploratory** (a candidate family added after the first results were seen, selection optimism applies). Deployment: the pipeline shipped to the app is chosen by the same inner-fold rule on the six out-of-fold tables with the extended candidate set; `scores/hybrid_final_hoffman.csv` holds the nested scores of the pipeline family that is deployed (the column `ruleset` says `A2` or `EXT`).
