All datasets are public except OpenOximetry, which is credentialed (used as derived aggregates only, never redistributed). Model weights trained on a restricted-licence dataset are flagged here and carry the terms below: **MTHS is CC BY-NC-ND (non-commercial research use only, no adaptations redistributed)** and **OpenOximetry is under the PhysioNet data use agreement**. The logistic chain weights (`app/data/weights.json`) are fit on Hoffman only. The shipped CNN weights (`app/data/hybrid_R_s0.json`, `hybrid_R_s1.json`, `hybrid_R_s2.json`, `app/data/oxynet.onnx`; hashes in `results/ml/FINAL_MODEL.json`) are fit on Hoffman, MTHS, BUT PPG and OpenOximetry rows plus pretraining on PhysioNet PTT-PPG, moshekam exercise, apnoea and BIDMC signals (corpus manifest: `results/ml/ssl_manifest.json`). No identifiable patient records were redistributed. The model does not train on, and the app does not store, any data from its users: camera frames are averaged in the browser and discarded. The optional "Download raw trace CSV" and "Copy debug summary" buttons write a file or clipboard text on the user's own device only.

**Compute handling.** Heavy training used a Daytona sandbox; OpenOximetry-derived arrays were uploaded only there (never to Kaggle) and stay on the sandbox and the analysis host (remove the sandbox with `python scripts/ml_daytona.py down`). Kaggle was used only for the HealthRing experiment, which touched public-licence data (HealthRing CC BY, Hoffman MIT) and no OpenOximetry or MTHS data. Checkpoints and run directories stay outside git.

**Minimum group size (5 people).** No committed table reports a group of fewer than 5 people from MTHS, BUT PPG or OpenOximetry (`MIN_GROUP` in `python/oxydrop_ml/openox_spo2.py`): a smaller group's aggregate is close to one person's result. In `results/ml/openox_spo2_skin.csv` the Monk 7+ group (2 people) and the ITA `unknown` group (4 people) keep only their people count and have `suppressed = True`; a dark-minus-light gap needs 5 people in both groups; `hr_vote_slow_pulse.csv` has no MTHS row (it was one person) and keeps the Hoffman rows (MIT data, one named slow-pulse case). `results/mths_spo2/per_person.csv` and `results/hr_validation/long_windows.csv` predate this branch, sit on `main` and are not changed here; they are the owner's call (see the licence action below).

## Shipped model weights: attribution and terms (owner decision, 2026-11-15)

The three CNN seed files (`app/data/hybrid_R_s0.json`, `hybrid_R_s1.json`, `hybrid_R_s2.json`) and the ONNX export (`app/data/oxynet.onnx`) are **derived models**. They were trained on MTHS (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) and on OpenOximetry (PhysioNet credentialed data use agreement), besides Hoffman (MIT), BUT PPG (CC BY 4.0) and the PhysioNet pretraining corpus. The weights contain no raw signal, no frame and no per-person record, and the repository publishes aggregates only for OpenOximetry and MTHS.

**Terms for these weight files (they are not covered by the MIT licence of the code):**

* **Non-commercial, research and education use only.** No commercial use, no clinical or diagnostic use, no sale or bundling into a paid product.
* **Attribution is required.** When you use or redistribute the weights, credit **MTHS** (Samavati et al., arXiv:2204.08989, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) and **OpenOximetry** (PhysioNet, credentialed dataset), and also Hoffman et al. 2022 (MIT), BUT PPG v2.0 (CC BY 4.0) and the PhysioNet pretraining sources listed above.
* **Do not redistribute the MTHS or OpenOximetry data** or per-person results from them. This repository does not.
* The same notice sits next to the files in `app/data/WEIGHTS_LICENSE.md` and in the README.

**Status of the decision.** The project owner (Arav) approved shipping the weights under these terms on 2026-11-15. This is the owner's decision, not a legal opinion. No written confirmation from the MTHS authors or the PhysioNet data owners is stored in this repository, so whether a trained network counts as an "adaptation" under NoDerivatives, and whether publishing weights is allowed under the OpenOximetry agreement, remain interpretations that the owner has accepted. If either data owner objects, remove the weights or retrain the shipped members without that dataset (a loss in skill is expected: the OpenOximetry warm-up is the largest single source of the CNN's skill, see `MODEL.md`). The logistic fallback chain (`app/data/weights.json`) is fit on Hoffman only and is unaffected.
