# Data sources and licences

| Dataset | Used for | Licence | Redistributed here? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UW Hoffman 2022**, "Smartphone Camera Oximetry in an Induced Hypoxemia Study" (npj Digital Medicine 2022), https://github.com/ubicomplab/oximetry-phone-cam-data | The only open phone-camera data with real SpO2 < 90. Fits the shipped weights and gives every Hoffman result (6 people, 12 hands). | **MIT**, Copyright (c) 2020 UW Ubicomp Lab | Yes: two recordings' per-frame mean RGB as parity fixtures (`tests/fixtures/`), two recordings as the in-app replay demo (`app/data/replay/`), and per-window scores. MIT notice retained. |
| **BUT PPG v2.0** (Brno University of Technology smartphone PPG database), PhysioNet, https://physionet.org/content/butppg/2.0.0/ | v2 heart-rate validation against ECG and subject-level cross-fit selection of the heart-rate gate (`scripts/validate_hr.py`, `scripts/hr_crossfit.py`, `docs/HR_VALIDATION.md`). Never used to fit the shipped weights. | **CC BY 4.0** | No. Only per-clip estimates and aggregate tables (`results/hr_validation/`), which contain the dataset's clip ID, subject number and reference rate. Cite the dataset when reusing them. |
| **MTHS** (smartphone fingertip video with SpO2, normoxic, 62 people) | Person-level test of the optical red/green/blue ratio-of-ratios (`scripts/mths_spo2.py`, `docs/SPO2_OPTICS.md`) and heart-rate validation. Negative result, never used to fit the shipped weights. | **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0** (from the dataset's "Dataset Licence.txt": Attribution, NonCommercial, NoDerivatives) | No. Only aggregate metrics (`results/mths_spo2/`) are published. Use of the data here is non-commercial research. Do not redistribute the data or adaptations of it. |
| **OpenOximetry** (PhysioNet, credentialed access; healthy volunteers, staircase hypoxia, arterial blood-gas SaO2, contact finger-clip red/IR PPG, not phone video) | **Used** (access is now active) for validation of the heart-rate-rise stage, a time-matched baseline, a fairness check by skin tone, and later model pretraining. Derived aggregates only. Raw files, per-window arrays and per-person tables stay on the analysis host or sandbox (Daytona only; never uploaded to Kaggle) and are never committed. | PhysioNet credentialed data use agreement (no redistribution). Cite the dataset when reusing the aggregates | **No.** Aggregate tables only (`results/openoximetry.md` on branch `openox-results`). Any model weights trained on OpenOximetry must be listed in this file and are not redistributed as data |
| **Altitude-chamber pilot (2025)**, 20 people, 1 Hz HR and SpO2, no optical signal (figshare) | An earlier reproduction of the "heart rate rises as SpO2 falls" link (zero-shot AUROC 0.88 on Hoffman windows). That claim has no self-contained rerun in this repository (UNSUPPORTED) and is not relied on. Not used to fit the shipped weights. | Licence not recorded in our notes; confirm at the source before reuse | No |
| **moshekam PPG exercise** (GitHub, Matlab/text files) | Exercise false-alarm test on normoxic people (63.4% of exercise windows flagged, 68.4% over the 13 subjects with a usable heart rate; raw extraction not reproducible here). Evaluation only. | **MIT**, Copyright (c) 2021 moshekam | No (only the per-window false-alarm counts, `results/scores/exercise_fp.csv`) |
| **HealthRing** (Zenodo, red/IR contact ring) | Source-domain experiment that was dropped (direction did not transfer). | **CC BY** | No |

All datasets are public except OpenOximetry, which is credentialed (used as derived aggregates only, never redistributed). Model weights trained on a restricted-licence dataset must be flagged here: **MTHS is CC BY-NC-ND (non-commercial research use only, no adaptations redistributed)** and **OpenOximetry is under the PhysioNet data use agreement**. The currently shipped weights (`app/data/weights.json`) are fit on Hoffman only. No identifiable patient records were redistributed. The model does not train on, and the app does not store, any data from its users: camera frames are averaged in the browser and discarded. The optional "Download raw trace CSV" and "Copy debug summary" buttons write a file or clipboard text on the user's own device only.
