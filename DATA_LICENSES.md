# Data sources and licences

| Dataset | Used for | Licence | Redistributed here? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UW Hoffman 2022**, "Smartphone Camera Oximetry in an Induced Hypoxemia Study" (npj Digital Medicine 2022), https://github.com/ubicomplab/oximetry-phone-cam-data | The only open phone-camera data with real SpO2 < 90. Fits the shipped weights and gives every Hoffman result (6 people, 12 hands). | **MIT**, Copyright (c) 2020 UW Ubicomp Lab | Yes: two recordings' per-frame mean RGB as parity fixtures (`tests/fixtures/`), two recordings as the in-app replay demo (`app/data/replay/`), and per-window scores. MIT notice retained. |
| **BUT PPG v2.0** (Brno University of Technology smartphone PPG database), PhysioNet, https://physionet.org/content/butppg/2.0.0/ | v2 heart-rate validation against ECG and subject-level cross-fit selection of the heart-rate gate (`scripts/validate_hr.py`, `scripts/hr_crossfit.py`, `docs/HR_VALIDATION.md`). Never used to fit the shipped weights. | **CC BY 4.0** | No. Only per-clip estimates and aggregate tables (`results/hr_validation/`), which contain the dataset's clip ID, subject number and reference rate. Cite the dataset when reusing them. |
| **MTHS** (smartphone fingertip video with SpO2, normoxic, 62 people) | Person-level test of the optical red/green/blue ratio-of-ratios (`scripts/mths_spo2.py`, `docs/SPO2_OPTICS.md`) and heart-rate validation. Negative result, never used to fit the shipped weights. | **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0** (from the dataset's "Dataset Licence.txt": Attribution, NonCommercial, NoDerivatives) | No. Only aggregate metrics (`results/mths_spo2/`) are published. Use of the data here is non-commercial research. Do not redistribute the data or adaptations of it. |
| **OpenOximetry** (PhysioNet, credentialed access) | **Not used.** Access was requested but never activated, so no OpenOximetry data or results appear anywhere in this project. | PhysioNet credentialed data use agreement | No |
| **Altitude-chamber pilot (2025)**, 20 people, 1 Hz HR and SpO2, no optical signal (figshare) | Reproducing the "heart rate rises as SpO2 falls" link (zero-shot AUROC 0.88 on Hoffman windows). Not used to fit the shipped weights. | Licence not recorded in our notes; confirm at the source before reuse | No |
| **moshekam PPG exercise** (GitHub, Matlab/text files) | Exercise false-alarm test on normoxic people (63% of exercise windows flagged). Evaluation only. | **MIT**, Copyright (c) 2021 moshekam | No (only the per-window false-alarm counts, `results/scores/exercise_fp.csv`) |
| **HealthRing** (Zenodo, red/IR contact ring) | Source-domain experiment that was dropped (direction did not transfer). | **CC BY** | No |

All datasets are public except OpenOximetry, which is credentialed and unused. No real patient records were used. The model does not train on, and the app does not store, any data from its users: camera frames are averaged in the browser and discarded. The optional "Download raw trace CSV" and "Copy debug summary" buttons write a file or clipboard text on the user's own device only.
