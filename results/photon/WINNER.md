## 8. Honesty items

1. **Post hoc.** `twin_gate` and `twin_gate_soft` were requested at 17:37 CT after the planner had read the outer twin_inverse and chain results; `flashgap_gate` at 17:15 CT after the planner read the FlashGap and OxyTrack outer results. The 10 dB threshold was fixed a priori and never tuned, but the decision to build a gate at all came from seeing that the physics path is flat under stress and the chain is not. None of these can be called a pre-registered win.
2. **Tie by construction at L0.** The shipped model equals chain_v1 on every clean window, so its L0 number carries no information beyond chain_v1's own and its interval is the chain's.
3. **A 3-number colour model is about as stress-robust as the physics path.** `ctl_dc_lr` (logistic regression on the three baseline-relative ln DC values) scores 0.692 at L3, 0.658 at L4, 0.599 at L5 and 0.646 at X1, against 0.719, 0.715, 0.647, 0.693 for `twin_inverse_noresid`. Its paired difference to the physics path includes 0 at L0 to L4 and X1 (`controls_log.md`). The physics inversion has not been shown to add accuracy under stress beyond the DC colour cue.
4. **The simulator favours baseline-relative features.** `stress.py` scales the whole recording (baseline minute included) by a constant attenuation, so first-minute-relative DC changes survive by construction; only the additive leak compresses them. The physics path's robustness is a property of this simulator, not evidence about a real distant-flash phone, whose auto-exposure and colour processing may break the cancellation.
5. **No posterior-width abstention.** The PhotonTwin-Inverse Laplace posterior width does not widen in weak light (SD 9.3 to 10.4 SpO2 points from L0 to L5, prior 15; kappa = 0 in 4 of 6 folds). Abstention under stress comes from an input-support gate (answer only if red and green SNR are above the 1 % quantile of the training windows) that was added after seeing the flat coverage of v2: post hoc, and it changes `answer` and `conf` only, never `pred` or TM.
6. **Optimistic development.** OxyTrack: four versions were scored on the outer folds during development (L0 0.673, 0.774, 0.815, 0.809); its L0 is not a pre-registered test. PhotonTwin-Inverse: trial configurations (encoder type, steps, Huber weight, saturation prior, residual grid) were compared by pooled leave-one-person-out TM on all six people before the grid was frozen; v1 was replaced by v2 after its held-out stress results were seen; so its L0 is optimistic. FlashGap: v2 and v3 (the primary `flashgap`) were designed after seeing v1 (and v2) held-out results. The deployed pipeline R is optimistic for the same reason.
7. **The gate's null controls are chain_v1's own nulls**, and single draws are noisy (global-permutation null of chain_v1: mean 0.506, SD 0.070 over 50 draws; 30 % of single draws above 0.55; 25 % of the first 20). See section 7.
8. **The gate fails at X1.** The white-noise SNR estimate is fooled by correlated noise and routes most X1 windows to the chain, which then answers at chance with high coverage. A real phone with motion or correlated noise could behave the same way.
9. **Forced-decision TM is not the whole story.** At L4 and L5 the shipped model answers (almost) nothing, and the TM win there is for the forced decision at full coverage. Its best TM at the levels it wins (L3 to L5: 0.715) stays far below the clean 0.796.
10. **Simulation.** The stress levels were built from a diffusion optics model with stated deviations (renderer sign of the image-source term, effective separation scale PATH_SCALE = 0.25 chosen from input-side SNR before any model was scored, quantisation 1/32 DN). The real iPhone behaviour is untested; no new phone was measured. FlashGap trained on the same simulator family.
11. **Small sample.** Six people, 12 recordings, 462 distinct person resamples; intervals are wide, differences of 0.05 are not resolved, and 5/6 people better gives sign-test p = 0.109 (6/6: 0.016).
12. **Code notes.** `optics.py` summed the Farrell dipole terms with the wrong sign in the committed draft; FEAT-002 fixed it (now `logaddexp`). `stress.py` evaluated its own corrected sum, so the stress levels were unaffected.
