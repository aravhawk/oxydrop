# OxySeq log (round-2 candidate R2D)

Generated by `python -m oxydrop_ml.photon.oxyseq`. Out-of-fold numbers (outer LOSO by person, both hands); every choice nested on the 5 training people (inner leave-one-training-person-out, seed 0 for the inner runs). Fit on clean training people plus the declared feature-space augmentation prior (module docstring); not refit per stress level. Stress levels are simulations (stress.py), applied to the held-out person.

Architecture: per-window linear gate head + gated learned-rate TTA of the 19 deltas + Linear(22->16) ReLU + GRU(16) + Linear(16+22->3) heads (hypoxia, SpO2 desaturation, quality/abstain). Training: Adam lr 0.003, wd 0.0001, batch 16 random crops of 32..128 windows, steps chosen from (25, 50, 100, 200) by inner CV; 3 seeds (0, 1, 2) for the final fit; shipped score = 3-seed ensemble.

## Disclosures

* Designed after round-1 outer results were seen (ROUND2.md, Amendment 7). The configuration (architecture, crop lengths, loss weights, augmentation prior) was fixed before the first run; see the development history for the one later change.
* The inner selection runs one seed (0); the chosen steps, hypoxia threshold and abstain tau are applied to the 3-seed ensemble.
* The augmentation is a feature-space prior, not a raw-RGB physics simulation (time budget); it is independent of stress.py.
* The TTA gate uses the label-free first-minute indicator (t + 10 s <= 60 s) as oxytrack does; it gates the adaptation only and is not a network input.

## Development history (disclosed researcher degree of freedom)

* v1 (steps grid 100, 200, 400): the inner selection chose 100, the grid minimum, in 6/6 folds (inner TM fell with more steps in every fold). Outer results were then seen: L0 TM 0.708, blank 0.500, L1 0.694, L2 0.656, L3 0.533, L4 0.503, L5 0.498, X1 0.508. Its nulls and notta were not run. Log and run output kept as `oxyseq_log_v1.md`, `oxyseq_run_v1.txt`; its score files were removed because v2 chose the same 100 steps in 6/6 folds and reproduced them (identical pred and answer on every window, scores within 5e-6).
* v2 (this file): the only change is the steps grid extended downward to (25, 50, 100, 200) because the v1 choice sat on the grid edge (an inner-CV signal); the change was nevertheless made after the v1 outer numbers were seen, so v2 carries that selection.
