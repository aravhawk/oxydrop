# OxyTrack log (photon candidate C)

Generated by `python -m oxydrop_ml.photon.oxytrack`. All numbers are out-of-fold (outer LOSO by person, both hands together); every choice is nested on the 5 training people (inner leave-one-training-person-out, selection metric TM bal. acc. of `pred`). Models are fit on CLEAN training people and not refit per stress level. Stress levels are simulations (python/oxydrop_ml/photon/stress.py), applied to the whole held-out recording only. Stress seed 0. 

Model: zero-mean random-walk desaturation state + 2 per-channel offsets (3-state Kalman filter), 2 observations per window (dHR and a projection of the 19 chain v1 FUSION deltas, nested between a ridge regression onto d and the chain v1 logistic direction; the deltas already include baseline-relative breathing rate (rr_amp, rr_ibi), RMSSD and SDNN, RSA amplitude was not added), observation gain attenuated by the measured per-window pulse SNR, label-free online-EM noise scales and offsets that adapt ONLY inside the first 60 s or on windows the filter already calls clearly normal (predictive P(s<90) < 0.10). L0 has no randomness, so the seed does not change L0 numbers.

Shipped models and smoothing: chain v1 (python/oxydrop/chain.py) is per-window with no smoothing; the hybrid (python/oxydrop_ml/hybrid.py) already applies a causal mean over k windows (tta.calibrate_and_smooth). `oxytrack_ema` is the smoothing control for chain v1.

Runtime of the run that wrote this file: 240 s.

## Development history (disclosed researcher degrees of freedom)

Four versions were run on the outer LOSO, each nested; the design changes were made after seeing earlier OUTER results, so the final numbers carry that (small-n) selection and are not a pre-registered test:

* v1: fus channel = ridge projection only, decision fixed at P(s<90) > 0.5, q_mult up to 4: L0 TM 0.673 (EMA control 0.798, chain v1 refit 0.796).
* v2: fus projection nested in {ridge, chain-v1 logistic direction}; decision threshold on P(s<90) nested (baselines.common.choose_thr, as the tuned baselines); q_mult up to 16: L0 TM 0.774, nosnr 0.810, noadapt 0.787, EMA 0.798. Under stress the answer rule kept 88-99 % coverage at chance accuracy (L3 0.943 at 0.536) because a wide posterior centred on the baseline still gives P(s<90) far from the tuned threshold, and the 80 % band covered only 57 % of clean windows.
* v3: q_mult grid extended to 64 (the inner selection sat on the edge, 16, in every fold); information gate added to `answer` (abstain when the posterior SD exceeds the 0.99 quantile of the inner-OOF SDs of the clean training people); 80 % band SD scaled so the inner-OOF band covers 80 % of the training windows. The score and pred (and so TM) are unaffected by the gate and the band scale. L0 TM 0.815 (nosnr 0.834, noadapt 0.817, EMA 0.798); q_mult = 64, again the grid edge, in every fold.
* v4 (this file): q_mult grid extended to 1024 (a near-flat dynamics prior, i.e. almost no smoothing, is now inside the grid); `conf` of the filter variants ranks windows that fail the information gate below all others (coverage curves only). Declared final before its outer results were seen.
