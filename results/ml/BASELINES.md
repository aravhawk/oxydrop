# Honest baselines under the common leave-one-subject-out harness

Generated by `python -m oxydrop_ml.baselines.run_baselines --all` (from `python/`, ML venv); every number below is read from `results/ml/baselines_P{1,2,3}.csv`. Regenerate with `bash scripts/reproduce_ml.sh baselines`. Per-window scores: `results/ml/scores/baseline_P1_hoffman.csv`, `baseline_P2_butppg.csv` (the MTHS per-window scores are not committed: MTHS is CC BY-NC-ND, they stay in the cache).

## Protocol (pre-registered, python/oxydrop_ml/PREREGISTRATION.md)

- Leave-one-subject-out: the subject is the group (both Hoffman hands together). No window-level split exists anywhere.
- Everything selected is selected by nested CV on the outer training people only: hyperparameters (XGBoost/LightGBM depth, leaves, trees; ridge alpha; chain C pairs), decision thresholds, abstention half-widths, the 90%-specificity threshold and the v2 SQI/ACF gate. `tests/test_baselines.py` checks that scrambling the held-out person changes none of them.
- Calibration: first 60 s of each Hoffman recording (30 s MTHS, first 6 BUT clips), label-free, the same personal baseline the CNN uses.
- Per row: pooled value with a 95% person-bootstrap interval, mean ± SD over held-out people, and the train-vs-held-out gap; long-format rows are in the CSVs.
- CSV schema (long format, one row per task / dataset / model / variant / metric): `pooled`, `pooled_lo`, `pooled_hi` (all out-of-fold windows pooled, 95% person bootstrap), `fold_mean`, `fold_sd`, `fold_lo`, `fold_hi`, `fold_min`, `fold_max`, `n_folds` (per held-out person), `train_mean`, `test_mean`, `gap`, `gap_sd`, `gap_lo`, `gap_hi` (train minus held-out, positive = over-fit; undefined for counts and signed bias). Rows named `*_diff_vs_*` / `mae_improvement_vs_train_mean` are paired person-bootstrap differences. `baselines_P1_perperson.csv` (Hoffman, MIT) and `baselines_P2_perperson.csv` (BUT PPG, CC BY) hold every fold (selected hyperparameters, thresholds, abstain half-widths). MTHS is CC BY-NC-ND (aggregates only): its people appear only as the aggregate spread (people, mean, SD, 10/25/50/75/90th percentiles) in `baselines_P2_spread.csv` and `baselines_P3_spread.csv`.
- Variants: `full` forced answer; `band_shipped` / `band_nested` abstention bands; `nolock_*` v2 abstains when the pulse is not locked; `spec90` threshold at 90% specificity on inner scores.
- P2 estimators have no fitted parameters, so their train-vs-held-out gap is sampling noise around zero, not over-fitting.
- The v1 chain refit reproduces `results/eval_v1_vs_v2.csv` (accuracy 0.8754, balanced accuracy 0.8733, AUROC 0.9475, TM accuracy 0.7962).

## P1: low-oxygen detection (reference SpO2 < 90), UW Hoffman, leave-one-person-out

5,644 windows (t >= 60 s) from 6 people; **57.48% of the evaluated windows are hypoxic, so "always alert" is the majority-class rule (accuracy 57.48%, balanced accuracy 0.500).** Primary metric: time-matched (TM) balanced accuracy at full coverage (a clock-only model scores 0.5 by construction). Intervals in square brackets are 95% person-bootstrap intervals (6 people resampled, B=1000) of the pooled value; "per person" is mean ± SD over the 6 held-out people; "gap" is train minus held-out for the same metric per fold (positive = over-fit), mean ± SD over folds. Chain features come from the raw Hoffman CSVs.

## Caveats

- Six Hoffman people (one dark-skinned) are the only open phone-camera recordings with real SpO2 below 90; all P1 intervals are wide and nothing here supports a claim about new people at scale.
- Hoffman accuracy is largely the shared protocol timeline: the clock-only model reaches high plain accuracy but 0.5 on the time-matched metric. TM numbers are the honest ones.
- Hyperparameter grids are small and fixed in code (`xgb_hand.py`, `chain_v1.py`, `p3.py`); selection optimism between the many variants is not removed by the intervals.
- Tree models on six people select hyperparameters from five inner folds of one person each; their inner scores are noisy.
- No frame timestamps exist in any dataset, so these baselines never see irregular sampling.
- MTHS is non-commercial research data (CC BY-NC-ND): only aggregates are committed.
