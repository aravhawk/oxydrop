# OxyDrop evaluation tables

Hoffman 2022 (UW), 6 people / 12 hands, leave-one-PERSON-out (nested), eval windows t >= 60 s (5,644), label = reference SpO2 < 90 (base rate 0.575). Generated by `scripts/make_eval.py` from `results/scores/`. Sensitivity (FNR = 1 - sens = missed hypoxemia) and specificity (FPR = 1 - spec = false alarms) are reported separately. The 6-person sample cannot resolve differences of a few points; the person-bootstrap CI for TINY vs clock accuracy overlaps (RESULTS.md: delta +3.7 pts, CI [-7, +16]).
