# Novelty and evidence audit

Literature checked **2026-11-15**. Citation keys match `references.bib`; direct DOI and inspected-source links appear below. “Candidate” means a potentially useful contribution within the reviewed literature, **not an established priority claim or measured improvement**. No “first” claim is supported by this review.

The user-specified CNN design is the paper's target architecture. The `main` snapshot used for this documentation (`092e12f`) still describes a logistic chain, negative optical-input experiments, and no OpenOximetry use. This document does not inspect or certify concurrent `ml-model*` work. Confirm the final model revision, dataset access, actual training runs, parameter count, conformal implementation, and deployment before changing proposed contributions to completed results. Earlier negative experiments must remain visible when reporting the CNN extension.

| Claim | Closest prior work | What is new / prior-work coverage | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smartphone fingertip camera can estimate oxygen saturation or flag low oxygen | Ding [@ding2019]; Hoffman [@hoffman2022] | **ALREADY COVERED.** Hardware accessibility and screening motivation are background, not novelty. | Reproduce a matched comparator on identical subjects, labels, windows, and saturation range; no superiority claim from unmatched published metrics. |
| Beat-level red/green ratio-of-ratios channels improve a waveform CNN | RGB ratio methods [@tian2022]; physiology-inspired networks [@mathew2023]; device calibration [@xuan2023] | **CANDIDATE 1.** Explicit beat-resolved AC/DC and ratio channels coupled to this multitask backbone. Optical ratios and physics-informed neural models are already covered. | Define amplitudes, DC estimates, beat alignment, causal interpolation, clipping/denominator gates, and missing-channel handling. Compare waveform-only, ratios-only, concatenated channels, and matched-size controls using frozen outer folds. Report the earlier failed optics experiment and whether CNN inputs overcome its failure. |
| Contrastive pretraining is novel for PPG or smartphone vital signs | Apple [@abbaspourazad2024]; PaPaGei [@pillai2025]; Vitals-CLIP [@mehmood2023] | **ALREADY COVERED.** Neither SSL, contrastive objectives, participant-aware learning, nor camera vital estimation is new. | Identify the exact objective, augmentations, positive pairs, and source cohorts. Do not call a small task model a foundation model without evidence of broad reuse. |
| SSL → ABG desaturation warm-up → smartphone multitask fine-tuning | PPG SSL above; OpenOximetry [@fong2025; @openox2025]; compact cross-sensor models [@samavati2026] | **CANDIDATE 2.** The specific staged training curriculum for this low-oxygen screen; no claim that transfer learning or ABG labels are new. | Show authorized real-data access, dataset versions, manifests, measured SaO₂ fields, synchronized waveform/sample pairs, and wavelength-to-camera mapping. Compare scratch, SSL-only, warm-up-only, reversed-order, and full curriculum. Mask unavailable HR/SpO₂ labels. Exclude every held-out identity from all training stages, including SSL; otherwise label the evaluation transductive. |
| Per-user baseline conditioning solves personalization and skin-tone bias | Device calibration [@xuan2023]; participant-aware representation learning [@abbaspourazad2024; @pillai2025] | **PARTLY COVERED / NARROW EXTENSION.** Conditioning this hybrid on a causal unlabeled baseline may be useful; it is distinct from camera calibration. Fairness is unproved. | Document baseline length, label-free inputs, rejected baselines, and whether calibration assumes initial normoxia. Compare no calibration, simple centering, and learned conditioning; test initially hypoxemic users. Never use future windows or held-out labels for adaptation. |
| Subject-nested stacking of the v1 physiological chain and CNN | Stacked generalization [@wolpert1992]; camera CNN comparator [@hoffman2022] | **CANDIDATE 3.** Testing whether this explicit physiological chain supplies complementary information to the learned optical model. Stacking itself is already covered. | Generate each meta-training score out of fold by person, with the base pipeline refit inside that fold. Select combiner and thresholds only inside outer training subjects; compare each base model and simple averaging. Report paired subject-level uncertainty, exercise false alarms, and time-matched controls. |
| Roughly 168k parameters, multitask HR/SpO₂, and on-phone inference | Compact vital estimators and TFLite [@samavati2026]; smartphone multivital networks [@mehmood2023] | **ALREADY COVERED IN PRINCIPLE.** ONNX/JavaScript packaging is an engineering contribution, not a new modeling principle or evidence of smallest size. | Count trainable/inference parameters from the exported artifact; verify Python/ONNX/JS parity, memory, latency, supported browsers, and the actual capture-to-decision path on named phones. |
| Conformal three-state output makes the screen reliable | General conformal framework [@angelopoulos2022]; clinical discussion [@vazquez2022] | **APPLICATION EXTENSION.** Mapping sets to these actions may distinguish the product; abstention and medical conformal prediction are already covered. | Specify nonconformity score, alpha, calibration unit/split, finite-sample quantile, and empty-set behavior. Separate quality-gate rejection from model abstention. Report marginal and class-conditional empirical set coverage, answer rate, selective risk, and low-oxygen misses including abstentions. No patient-level guarantee from naive window calibration. |
| LOSO, nested CV, and preregistration make performance conclusive | Subject-held-out camera evaluation [@hoffman2022]; out-of-fold stacking [@wolpert1992] | **ALREADY COVERED / EVALUATION STRENGTH.** A public, timestamped full-pipeline protocol can improve auditability; six people still limit inference. | Keep both hands and every window of a person together. Freeze feature/model/hyperparameter choices before outer evaluation; publish protocol hash/time and all deviations. A plan written after seeing the outcomes is retrospective, not preregistration. Bootstrap people, show each subject, and avoid treating window count as participant count. |
| OpenOximetry makes phone predictions ABG-validated | OpenOximetry common data model and release [@fong2025; @openox2025] | **UNSUPPORTED AS STATED.** Training on arterial labels in another modality does not validate phone outputs against arterial labels. | Identify actual phone–arterial paired test data. Distinguish measured SaO₂ from PaO₂ and SpO₂; report sensor domain shift and reference timing. Do not describe the general OpenOximetry release as smartphone video data. |
| Calibration and abstention eliminate pigmentation disparities | Sjoding [@sjoding2020]; Fawzy [@fawzy2022]; FDA draft [@fda2025] | **UNSUPPORTED.** Bias mitigation is a research objective, not a result. | Prospective diverse phone cohort with objective pigmentation measurements, paired arterial reference where feasible, prespecified subgroup denominators, error/coverage/false-negative analysis, and uncertainty. Race is not a pigmentation measurement. |
| Clinically safe or FDA-compliant low-oxygen diagnosis | Dedicated hardware study [@tayfur2019]; FDA draft [@fda2025] | **UNSUPPORTED.** A hackathon screen and offline benchmarks do not establish clinical validation, clearance, or compliance. | Device-appropriate prospective validation, human-factors assessment, defined intended use, and applicable regulatory review. “Normal” cannot establish absence of hypoxemia, especially when the initial baseline is already abnormal. |

The strongest **candidate** claims are rows 2, 4, and 6: the beat-resolved representation, the staged arterial-to-camera curriculum, and the personalized physiological/neural hybrid. The weakest novelty claims are generic camera/CNN oximetry and compact multitask on-phone inference, both directly anticipated. Conformal action mapping is a useful supporting application claim, subject to the calibration limitations above.

For split conformal with one independent score per subject, rank `ceil((n_cal + 1) * (1 - alpha))` is 6 at 90% coverage with five calibration subjects, requiring an uninformative conservative fallback [@angelopoulos2022]. This mathematical implication motivates a justified grouped design or more calibration participants; it is not an OxyDrop result.
