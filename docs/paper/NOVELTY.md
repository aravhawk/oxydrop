# Novelty and evidence audit

Literature checked **2026-11-15**. Citation keys match `references.bib`; direct DOI and inspected-source links appear below. “Candidate” means a potentially useful contribution within the reviewed literature, **not an established priority claim or measured improvement**. No “first” claim is supported by this review.

**Update for the implemented model (added after PR #6, `ml-model`, reviewed at `a23947d`).** The first version of this audit was written against `main` (`092e12f`), which had a logistic chain only. It was then reconciled with the CNN work of PR #6: the 167,588-parameter OxyNet (three seeds), SimCLR-style contrastive pretraining on 299,754 windows from 212 people, a supervised warm-up on OpenOximetry contact-sensor SaO2, the hybrid with the v1 chain, a Mondrian conformal three-state output, and the pre-registered leave-one-person-out audit (`python/oxydrop_ml/PREREGISTRATION.md`, `MODEL.md`, `WHITEPAPER.md`, `results/ml/*.csv`). The section [Status against the ml-model results](#status-against-the-ml-model-results) below states what those runs show. **The measured result is that the pre-registered primary metric was not beaten, so every candidate below is an implemented design with no demonstrated accuracy win.** The earlier negative optical-input experiments stay visible: physics channels gave no measured gain (see the status section). The OpenOximetry and MTHS weights carry licence limits described in `DATA_LICENSES.md`.

| Smartphone fingertip camera can estimate oxygen saturation or flag low oxygen | Ding [@ding2019]; Hoffman [@hoffman2022] | **ALREADY COVERED.** Hardware accessibility and screening motivation are background, not novelty. | Reproduce a matched comparator on identical subjects, labels, windows, and saturation range; no superiority claim from unmatched published metrics. |

Optical ratios and physics-informed neural models are already covered. | Define amplitudes, DC estimates, beat alignment, causal interpolation, clipping/denominator gates, and missing-channel handling. Compare waveform-only, ratios-only, concatenated channels, and matched-size controls using frozen outer folds. Report the earlier failed optics experiment and whether CNN inputs overcome its failure. |
| Contrastive pretraining is novel for PPG or smartphone vital signs | Apple [@abbaspourazad2024]; PaPaGei [@pillai2025]; Vitals-CLIP [@mehmood2023] | **ALREADY COVERED.** Neither SSL, contrastive objectives, participant-aware learning, nor camera vital estimation is new. | Identify the exact objective, augmentations, positive pairs, and source cohorts. Do not call a small task model a foundation model without evidence of broad reuse. |
| SSL → ABG desaturation warm-up → smartphone multitask fine-tuning | PPG SSL above; OpenOximetry [@fong2025; @openox2025]; compact cross-sensor models [@samavati2026] | **CANDIDATE 2 (implemented, no accuracy win over the chain).** The specific staged training curriculum for this low-oxygen screen; no claim that transfer learning or ABG labels are new. | Show authorized real-data access, dataset versions, manifests, measured SaO₂ fields, synchronized waveform/sample pairs, and wavelength-to-camera mapping. (ml-model: access is active and 34 OpenOximetry people were held out of every stage; the data are contact red/infrared PPG, not phone video; raw files are never committed.) Compare scratch, SSL-only, warm-up-only, reversed-order, and full curriculum. Mask unavailable HR/SpO₂ labels. Exclude every held-out identity from all training stages, including SSL; otherwise label the evaluation transductive. |
| Per-user baseline conditioning solves personalization and skin-tone bias | Device calibration [@xuan2023]; participant-aware representation learning [@abbaspourazad2024; @pillai2025] | **PARTLY COVERED / NARROW EXTENSION.** Conditioning this hybrid on a causal unlabeled baseline may be useful; it is distinct from camera calibration. Fairness is unproved. | Document baseline length, label-free inputs, rejected baselines, and whether calibration assumes initial normoxia. Compare no calibration, simple centering, and learned conditioning; test initially hypoxemic users. Never use future windows or held-out labels for adaptation. |
| Subject-nested stacking of the v1 physiological chain and CNN | Stacked generalization [@wolpert1992]; camera CNN comparator [@hoffman2022] | **CANDIDATE 3 (implemented, tie with the chain).** Testing whether this explicit physiological chain supplies complementary information to the learned optical model. Stacking itself is already covered. | Generate each meta-training score out of fold by person, with the base pipeline refit inside that fold. Select combiner and thresholds only inside outer training subjects; compare each base model and simple averaging. Report paired subject-level uncertainty, exercise false alarms, and time-matched controls. |
| 167,588 parameters per seed (three seeds), multitask HR/SpO₂, and on-phone inference | Compact vital estimators and TFLite [@samavati2026]; smartphone multivital networks [@mehmood2023] | **ALREADY COVERED IN PRINCIPLE.** ONNX/JavaScript packaging is an engineering contribution, not a new modeling principle or evidence of smallest size. | Count trainable/inference parameters from the exported artifact; verify Python/ONNX/JS parity, memory, latency, supported browsers, and the actual capture-to-decision path on named phones. (ml-model: 167,588 parameters counted, JS parity tests in `npm test`, about 50 to 58 ms per window on a laptop in Node; no phone latency or phone validation yet, see `results/ml/DEPLOY.md`.) |
| Conformal three-state output makes the screen reliable | General conformal framework [@angelopoulos2022]; clinical discussion [@vazquez2022] | **APPLICATION EXTENSION.** Mapping sets to these actions may distinguish the product; abstention and medical conformal prediction are already covered. | Specify nonconformity score, alpha, calibration unit/split, finite-sample quantile, and empty-set behavior. Separate quality-gate rejection from model abstention. Report marginal and class-conditional empirical set coverage, answer rate, selective risk, and low-oxygen misses including abstentions. No patient-level guarantee from naive window calibration. (ml-model: done in `results/ml/CONFORMAL.md`. Class-conditional (Mondrian) coverage holds approximately pooled and fails per person: one held-out person has 51.3% of hypoxic windows called Normal.) |
| LOSO, nested CV, and preregistration make performance conclusive | Subject-held-out camera evaluation [@hoffman2022]; out-of-fold stacking [@wolpert1992] | **ALREADY COVERED / EVALUATION STRENGTH.** A public, timestamped full-pipeline protocol can improve auditability; six people still limit inference. | Keep both hands and every window of a person together. Freeze feature/model/hyperparameter choices before outer evaluation; publish protocol hash/time and all deviations. (ml-model: `PREREGISTRATION.md` was committed as `3e708fe` before any CNN result, with five amendments listed in the file.) A plan written after seeing the outcomes is retrospective, not preregistration. Bootstrap people, show each subject, and avoid treating window count as participant count. |
| OpenOximetry makes phone predictions ABG-validated | OpenOximetry common data model and release [@fong2025; @openox2025] | **UNSUPPORTED AS STATED.** Training on arterial labels in another modality does not validate phone outputs against arterial labels. | Identify actual phone–arterial paired test data. (ml-model: none exists; OpenOximetry SaO2 results are contact-sensor only.) Distinguish measured SaO₂ from PaO₂ and SpO₂; report sensor domain shift and reference timing. Do not describe the general OpenOximetry release as smartphone video data. |
| Calibration and abstention eliminate pigmentation disparities | Sjoding [@sjoding2020]; Fawzy [@fawzy2022]; FDA draft [@fda2025] | **UNSUPPORTED.** Bias mitigation is a research objective, not a result. | Prospective diverse phone cohort with objective pigmentation measurements (ml-model: only one dark-skinned Hoffman participant, so no phone pigmentation conclusion; the OpenOximetry skin-tone strata are contact-sensor only), paired arterial reference where feasible, prespecified subgroup denominators, error/coverage/false-negative analysis, and uncertainty. Race is not a pigmentation measurement. |
| Clinically safe or FDA-compliant low-oxygen diagnosis | Dedicated hardware study [@tayfur2019]; FDA draft [@fda2025] | **UNSUPPORTED.** A hackathon screen and offline benchmarks do not establish clinical validation, clearance, or compliance. | Device-appropriate prospective validation, human-factors assessment, defined intended use, and applicable regulatory review. “Normal” cannot establish absence of hypoxemia, especially when the initial baseline is already abnormal. |

The **candidate** designs are rows 2, 4, and 6: the beat-resolved representation, the staged arterial-to-camera curriculum, and the personalized physiological/neural hybrid. The ml-model runs found no accuracy win for any of them on the pre-registered metric (next section), so they are described as implemented designs, not as demonstrated improvements. The weakest novelty claims are generic camera/CNN oximetry and compact multitask on-phone inference, both directly anticipated. Conformal action mapping is a useful supporting application claim, subject to the calibration limitations above.

For split conformal with one independent score per subject, rank `ceil((n_cal + 1) * (1 - alpha))` is 6 at 90% coverage with five calibration subjects, requiring an uninformative conservative fallback [@angelopoulos2022]. This mathematical implication motivates a justified grouped design or more calibration participants; it is not an OxyDrop result.

## Status against the ml-model results

All values below are from the repository files named in each line (leave-one-person-out on six Hoffman people unless stated; 95% person-bootstrap intervals; ablation rows other than the first are grouped 10-fold, not LOSO, as labelled in `MODEL.md`). They are recorded-data results, not phone validation.

* **Primary metric P1 (time-matched balanced accuracy, `results/ml/baselines_P1.csv`, `hybrid_p1_final.csv`, `cnn_vs_baselines_paired.csv`): not beaten.** Best baseline, the shipped v1 logistic chain, 0.796 [0.635, 0.856]. CNN alone 0.739 [0.574, 0.811] (three-seed ensemble 0.754, paired difference to the chain -0.042 [-0.131, 0.005]). Hybrid, nested (unbiased estimate of the selection procedure) 0.779 [0.638, 0.831], paired difference -0.017 [-0.103, 0.069]. Deployed pipeline R 0.809 [0.644, 0.863] is optimistic because it was chosen after the outer scores were seen. A chain refit with the same levers and no CNN scores 0.816 [0.674, 0.869]. All intervals overlap: ties. The model beats only weak baselines (textbook ratio-of-ratios 0.535, clock 0.495, which is near chance by construction on this metric).
* **Candidate 1, beat-level ratio channels:** removing the physics channels changes P1 by -0.008, inside the seed SD of 0.019: no measured gain.
* **Candidate 2, staged curriculum:** removing the OpenOximetry warm-up lowers P1 from 0.739 to 0.577 [0.507, 0.629], the largest effect in the study; removing the self-supervised stage gives 0.726 [0.542, 0.821], inside the seed SD. The person-level intervals of the full and no-OpenOximetry rows still overlap with six people. A reversed-order variant was not run.
* **Candidate 3, stacking with the chain:** the hybrid ties the chain and has lower point estimates than the chain-only control (above). It is kept by design, not because it wins.
* **Baseline conditioning:** removing it lowers P1 by 0.028 and widens the train-versus-test gap (+0.105 against -0.015).
* **Heart rate (P2, exploratory):** within 5 bpm 0.480 [0.429, 0.534] against 0.419 [0.366, 0.481] for the spectral estimator, paired +0.061 [0.013, 0.107]. The rule was chosen post hoc and the gain comes from BUT PPG.
* **Conformal three-state output (`results/ml/CONFORMAL.md`):** at alpha 0.10 the deployed pipeline answers 74.5% of windows at 89.5% accuracy against 92.5% at 90.3% for the chain-only control; it does not beat the chain at matched coverage. Pooled set coverage is near target and fails per person.
* **OpenOximetry (`results/ml/OPENOX_SPO2.md`):** contact-sensor SaO2 MAE 2.94 for the CNN, 3.07 for textbook ratio-of-ratios, 7.38 for the train mean. Not a phone test and not arterial validation of phone output.
* **Not done:** real-phone validation, any phone pigmentation measurement, device-stratified phone analysis. Only six hypoxic phone-camera people exist.
